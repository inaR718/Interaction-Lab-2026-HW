When I look up at a clear blue sky I feel a sense of peace yet existentialism. like the comfort of everything is so small in hindsight, but at the same time WOW everything is so small.. What is the meaning of it all? But peace comes more than existentialism because I like having meaning, even if it's not important in the grand scheme of things. and when i look at a clear blue sky, i think of what my mom used to tell me, "autumn skies are the prettiest". It's just a throwaway comment, not really deeper than what was said, but I thought the sentiment was beautiful and it just reminds me that things changing and time passing can be lovely. As much as I want to be stuck in summer, autumn skies will come and it will be beautiful. I think there's also something about a clear sky that makes me feel so light inside, like I'm floating.
