When I look into the endless blue sky I can't help but think about where the stars are. Like where they would be if it was night right now. I like to find the moon in the daytime. There's an infinite plane of space out there and we can only see a single particle of it. It makes me think of how recorded history doesnt even make up 1/100th of human existence, how the big bang can be compared to making a spark and how all our lifespans and even the earth’s lifespan lives inside that single moment of making a spark, how it would be really cool and really scary if the sky was kind of like an ocean. If creatures can live in the sky like an ecosystem. Sky whales and stuff. Unfortunately that isn’t the case. It's comforting to know that my imagination is still as wild as when I was a child. It's terrifying to think that maybe I will never be fully grown, and stay the same as I was all those years ago. If anything, the sky makes me think of a giant blanket covering the earth, both protecting us and preventing us from reaching our true potential. I dunno if that's a good thing or not.
