I feel like people have the wrong priorities in life. When you scale out your view of the world, you start to see how much of what we stress over is ultimately nothing. Whenever I can view the sky (or anything that lets me “zoom out”) it’s a reminder of what life actually is in its purest or most primal state. Tbh it’s probably why I have such a hard time prioritizing things like academics and school, it’s just hard to make them seem valuable when you really look at life that way. Life is a lot simpler than we make it out to be
